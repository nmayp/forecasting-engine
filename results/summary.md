# Benchmark summary (locked holdout, as of 25 Oct 2026)

Every number comes from the lab's locked holdout or a pre-declared check set, with publication lags audited (no look-ahead).
Inputs: `results/lab/bench.json` (open-model rows), `results/lab/trials.jsonl` (champion ledger, slimmed), `results/gifteval/`,
`results/cascade/`. The full 65-layer table is regenerated by `python -m forecasting_engine.lab.scoreboard` into `results/scoreboard.md`.

## Headline

On a locked holdout the engine is ahead of the best open foundation model on **43 of 65 layers**, and ahead of Chronos-2 on **58 of 65**.

## 1. Forecasting against open foundation models (locked holdout)

Skill = error reduction against a strict reference forecast (higher is better). Best open model = the best of Chronos-2 (Amazon), TiRex-2 (NX-AI)
and Toto 2.0 (Datadog), each in its best fair configuration.

| Layer | What is forecast | Engine | Best open model | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Energy | hourly electricity load | 51.9 % | 34.5 % (Toto 2.0) | +17.4 pts |
| Economy | euro-area inflation (monthly) | 37.8 % | 27.2 % (TiRex-2) | +10.7 pts |
| Energy | daily electricity load | 37.5 % | 28.6 % (Chronos-2) | +8.9 pts |
| Energy | hourly power price | 40.5 % | 32.4 % (Toto 2.0) | +8.1 pts |
| Transport | daily air traffic | 44.4 % | 37.4 % (Chronos-2) | +6.9 pts |
| Water | weekly water layer | 42.7 % | 35.8 % (Toto 2.0) | +6.9 pts |
| Energy | hourly wind and solar output | 55.8 % | 49.4 % (TiRex-2) | +6.4 pts |
| Economy | national inflation (monthly) | 35.4 % | 30.9 % (Toto 2.0) | +4.6 pts |
| Economy | unemployment | 12.1 % | 9.0 % (Toto 2.0) | +3.2 pts |
| Tourism | monthly tourism | 36.7 % | 35.1 % (Toto 2.0) | +1.6 pts |

Also ahead (smaller margins): fuel prices, industrial production, retail, ports, social unrest, security, news flow, cyber vulnerabilities, air quality,
wildfire. Not ahead: health (weekly flu-like illness, -2.3 pts), two long economic series, shipping chokepoints, and two financial-market series that are
close to random walks.

Notes: margins on several layers are under 1 point; every driver's publication delay was audited and leaks were fixed before these numbers (some wins were
withdrawn in that audit). Most forecasts are ensembles that include open models (Toto 2.0, FlowState, Chronos-2 or TiRex-2 as members next to the engine's
own cross-layer models); a layer counts as won only if the ensemble beats the best single open model, and the open models' own forecasts are never counted
as the engine's lead. A blending round with weights chosen on development only (9 holdout looks, 4 accepted) left the counts unchanged and moved the mean margin
over the best open model from +1.31 to +1.39 pts. Cross-layer inputs add little on top of a foundation model plus a layer's own drivers (98 candidates on
20 layers, 3 accepted); no claim should rest on them.

## 2. GIFT-Eval (Salesforce's public forecasting benchmark, 97 datasets)

| Model | MASE (lower is better) | CRPS (lower is better) |
|---|---|---|
| **Blend of four open models (this repo)** | **0.675** | **0.462** |
| Toto 2.0 (model card) | 0.699 | 0.478 |
| Chronos-2 | 0.698 | 0.485 |
| TimesFM 2.5 | 0.705 | 0.490 |
| TiRex-2 | 0.697 | 0.478 |

Geometric mean of metric / Seasonal Naive over all 97 configurations, as on the leaderboard. Own run of the official benchmark, not submitted to the public
leaderboard. The blend is Toto 2.0, Chronos-2, TiRex-2 (GIFT-Eval-clean checkpoint) and TimesFM 2.5, zero-shot, each in its authors' notebook setup; per
(domain x term) group the member subset and combiner (mean, median, trimmed mean) are chosen on validation windows placed before the test region only. The blend
beats the best single member by about 3 % on both metrics. Per-config rows: `results/gifteval/all_results.csv`; selection: `results/gifteval/selection.json`.

## 3. Cascades: real multi-layer shocks

- Cascade benchmark: 29 historical multi-layer events (164 cells; 21 development events, 8 locked check events). On the development events the engine's
  error is 6.4 % lower than the best open model. Cell results: `results/cascade/`.
- Shock replays: 41 of 99 past shocks reproduced (cause and effect in the right direction and size band).
